## 🔬 Research

### Data Science and AI applied to Small and Medium Retail Businesses
Undergraduate research project (PIBICT/CEETEPS 2026/2027) at FATEC Votorantim, running from September 2026 to August 2027. The goal is to help small and medium retailers in the Sorocaba metropolitan region make better decisions using their own data.

**What I'm building:**
- An open-source system to collect, convert, and clean business data
- An analysis model with management metrics for small businesses
- An executive dashboard to support decision-making
- An AI-based guidance system and an implementation guide for SMEs
- A scientific article and final report

**Stack:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib
